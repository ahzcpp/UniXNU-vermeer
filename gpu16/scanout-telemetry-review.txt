Telemetry follow-up to Claude's scanout client revision3

Hardware comparison: test170 (default options off) produced 58.7108 FPS in
the scripted Settings-scroll phase; test171 (only pace60 enabled) produced
57.5302 FPS. Both screenshots were normal and neither short benchmark had
app memory-pressure kills, recover or panic. Keep pacing off for ordinary
use while validating it; a 60-valued option is not itself a frame-rate fix.

PP/PV were sent through streamText7 alongside PF. At served frame601, PP's
600 intervals and PF's 120-frame progress period coincide. PF overwrites
the optional diagnostic before the periodic kernel observer usually reads
it. Absence of PP in the host log therefore does not prove pacing was off.

This follow-up changes only reporting: optional PP/PV use the existing
streamText/report8 channel and PF stays on report7. PC reports the parsed
pace/verify options once when opening the client. Both helpers and the
Client implementation remain byte-identical to revision3. Render, wait,
COMPLETE, verifier bounds and diagnostic attempt limits are unchanged.
No kernel archive, firmware, process privilege or physical refresh policy
changes are included. Report8 can still be replaced by a later phase report
after the serving client exits; these are observations, not health flags.

The patch was applied locally to the exact qualified revision3 header; the
new header SHA is a6bde0d561427bf21fd0d8c0360ed78127d3e6e24c2916670d9597de68c09907.
Real SDK compilation and device observation are pending. Do not mark this
fix verified until PC and bounded PP/PV lines are actually captured.
